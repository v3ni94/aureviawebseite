For Dentists

Factoring for dentists: more financial headroom between treatment and payment.

The outside dental lab issues its invoice as soon as the work is finished. Your patient pays once the restoration is fitted, and often only after their insurer or allowance office has responded. Weeks can pass in between, and you bridge that gap out of your own funds, in precisely your most revenue-intensive cases. AUREVIA addresses exactly this timing gap.

Starting point

Typical liquidity situations

Lab and material costs arise early in the treatment process, long before the patient's receivable falls due.

High-value prosthetics and implantology produce large individual invoices with a correspondingly large share to be advanced.

Treatment and cost plans have a long lead time. Months often pass between drawing up the plan, approval, treatment and payment.

The more high-value prosthetics you do, the more capital you tie up. In this constellation, growth initially worsens liquidity instead of improving it.

Modernisation costs money upfront: scanners, CBCT, treatment units, CAD/CAM, digitalisation.

Impact

What AUREVIA changes for you

  • Open receivables become a source of financing for material, lab costs and practice development.
  • Less dependence on rigid credit lines for short-term working-capital peaks.
  • Transparent purchase conditions instead of an unclear blended calculation.
  • Payment arrangements with patients can be structured within the agreed framework, an argument in the consultation, without you carrying the risk.
  • AUREVIA can be used as a specialised complement to existing billing and practice processes.
Process

How it works in a dental practice

Your billing under the GOZ fee schedule stays exactly as it is. After onboarding, you submit the invoice digitally along with the information needed for review, sensibly bundled once treatment is complete rather than individually after every appointment. AUREVIA checks the purchase conditions and shows you the terms; you decide. Co-payment shares owed by statutory-insurance patients are receivables against the patient and can be submitted just like purely private services. Rework and warranty remain your responsibility as before, governed by the treatment agreement.

Legal framework

What is legally required

As in human medicine: assigning a dentist's fee receivable to a third party requires the patient's express, prior and written consent (Section 203 of the German Criminal Code, the professional codes of the regional dental associations, settled case law of the Federal Court of Justice). AUREVIA provides you with suitable wording for the medical history form and treatment agreement, along with the process for obtaining consent at reception without extra effort.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions from dentists

Are statutory (BEMA) services also purchased?

The focus is on private dental services under the GOZ fee schedule, co-payment shares and self-pay services. We advise individually on your specific service structure.

What about the patient's share of the cost for dentures?

The co-payment share is a receivable against the patient and is therefore generally eligible for purchase.

What happens in warranty cases?

Rework and defect-liability rights remain governed by the treatment agreement. Where a receivable is reduced for this reason, it is corrected or reassigned back under the contractual rules; credit protection covers creditworthiness risk, not performance defects.
