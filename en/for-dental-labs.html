For Dental Labs

Factoring for dental labs: produce today. Receive liquidity sooner.

The gold has been paid for, the technicians have been paid, the work has gone out, and there is still nothing in the account. Dental labs pre-finance twice over: once for materials, once for their client practices' payment terms. Months regularly pass in between, for materials whose prices you don't control. AUREVIA shortens that gap.

Starting point

Typical liquidity situations

Precious metals, ceramics, zirconia and implant components are upfront outlays, paid for at purchase, reimbursed months later.

Staff costs run independently of when customers pay. Master dental technicians and qualified specialists are scarce and expensive to retain.

Few clients, but large ones: if a regular client practice fails, it is not one invoice that is lost, but a substantial share of revenue.

CAD/CAM, scanners, milling units and 3D printing demand continuous investment in a market that presupposes digital production.

A new major client first increases capital tie-up and default risk; the return comes later.

Impact

What AUREVIA changes for you

  • Receivables become a working-capital instrument instead of a waiting room for your money.
  • Growth does not automatically have to be financed through a higher overdraft line.
  • Liquidity develops in step with your receivables portfolio.
  • Under non-recourse factoring, the default risk transfers to AUREVIA within the agreed limits, given your debtor structure, typically the strongest argument.
  • The ongoing credit-limit review of your client practices acts as an early-warning system: if a limit is reduced, you know before the payment problems become visible.
Process

How it works in a dental lab

You invoice your client practices as usual and add the assignment notice. Before the first purchase against a practice, a debtor credit limit is reviewed; within this limit, non-recourse factoring provides credit protection, beyond it, it does not. You learn the limit in advance, not only in insolvency proceedings. Complaints and rework remain part of the contracted work and therefore your responsibility.

Legal framework

What is legally required

In the purely commercial relationship between lab and practice, the physician's duty of confidentiality under Section 203 of the German Criminal Code does not apply in the same way as it does to patient receivables. If your invoices or order documents nonetheless contain patient-related information, the principle of data minimisation applies: only the data required for the purchase, review and collection of the receivable is transmitted. Any existing blanket assignment (Globalzession) in favour of your bank must be reviewed before the contract is concluded.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions from lab owners

Will my client practices find out?

Under disclosed factoring, yes, the assignment notice appears on the invoice. In B2B business this is a routine process that, as a rule, does not strain the business relationship.

What if a practice raises a complaint about the work?

Defect claims and rework remain your responsibility. Credit protection (del credere) covers insolvency and unwillingness to pay, not legitimate objections to the contracted work.

Can I exclude individual clients?

Yes, under the agreed partial-portfolio model you define the group of debtors. Conversely, no credit protection applies to clients that are not submitted.
