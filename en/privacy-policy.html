2. Data Protection Officer

Once appointed, our Data Protection Officer can be reached at: [Name/firm, address, email]. For a future factoring institution with extensive processing of special categories of personal data, an appointment is generally required.

3. Principles and purposes of processing

We process personal data only to the extent necessary for operating this website and handling enquiries. Further purposes, in particular the initiation and conduct of business relationships as well as the review and processing of factoring transactions, will only apply once the future project company is founded and commences business operations.

4. Visiting the website / server log files

When you visit the website, technically necessary data is processed, in particular IP address, date and time, the URL accessed, referrer, browser and device information, and status codes. The purpose is to provide the website securely and reliably. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in secure and functional website operation. Retention period: [log retention period]. Hosting provider: [provider, address, server location, once selected]. A data processing agreement under Article 28 GDPR is in place with the provider.

5. Contact by email and phone

This website does not include a contact form. If you contact us using the email address or phone number provided, we process the data you share with us, in particular your email address, name (if provided), and the content of your message, to process and respond to your enquiry. The legal basis, depending on context, is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual and contractual communication, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other business enquiries. Retention period: until the purpose no longer applies, plus statutory retention periods.

6. Registration and customer portal

This website currently does not offer registration or a customer portal. Once a customer portal is introduced, we will supplement this notice with the master, company, contact, authentication, usage, contract and transaction data processed in that context, along with the respective purposes and legal bases.

7. Identification, anti-money-laundering prevention and regulatory obligations

Once the future project company commences business operations, it will, to the extent legally required, process identity data, representation data and data on beneficial owners, as well as further compliance data, in order to fulfil regulatory, anti-money-laundering and commercial-law obligations. The legal basis would in particular be Article 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with the applicable provisions of the German Anti-Money Laundering Act (GwG), the German Banking Act (KWG), the German Fiscal Code (AO) and the German Commercial Code (HGB). [Specific provisions and deadlines to be added by Legal/DPO before commencing business.]

8. Receivables and debtor data

In connection with future factoring enquiries and contracts, data on receivables, debtors, invoices, payment status and the underlying services will be processed. The principle of data minimisation under Article 5(1)(c) GDPR applies. The legal basis for processing debtor data would be Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR, for the purpose of collecting the purchased receivable. Debtors will be informed pursuant to Article 14 GDPR via [method to be specified, e.g. a notice on the invoice referring to this Privacy Policy].

9. Special categories of personal data / health data

Invoices in the healthcare context may contain information from which health data can be inferred, such as diagnosis, treatment or prescription details. Such data is subject to Article 9 GDPR and may only be processed under strict conditions.

Once the future project company purchases receivables, it would process such data on the basis of the explicit consent of the data subject pursuant to Article 9(2)(a) GDPR. This consent would be obtained from and documented by clients before assignment of the receivable; it also covers the release from the duty of confidentiality under Section 203 of the German Criminal Code, to the extent required for the assignment and collection. Without valid consent, the receivable could not be purchased. Data subjects may withdraw consent given at any time with effect for the future.

This is the legally most sensitive section of the entire website. Before commencing business, the specific processing model, the scope of transmitted service data, redaction/pseudonymisation, and the technical and organisational separation of billing and diagnosis data must be defined by the Data Protection Officer and legal counsel.

10. Creditworthiness and risk review

To review future factoring enquiries, company, receivables, debtor, payment and creditworthiness data would be processed, and information may be obtained from credit reporting agencies where applicable. Provider: [credit reporting agency/agencies, address]. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is securing the assumed risk.

11. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effect based solely on automated processing within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.

12. Cookies and consent management

This website currently does not use cookies or comparable technologies. Should technically necessary cookies be used in the future, this will be based on Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications-Digital-Services-Data-Protection Act (TDDDG), to the extent they are required for the digital service expressly requested. Non-essential analytics, marketing or third-party technologies would only be activated after consent pursuant to Section 25(1) TDDDG in conjunction with Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, via a consent management tool.

13. Web analytics and marketing

This website currently does not use any web analytics or marketing tools. Once tools are used, we will document, for each tool, the provider, categories of data, purpose, legal basis, retention period, recipients, third-country transfers and how to withdraw consent, individually in this section.

14. Embedded services

This website currently does not embed external fonts, maps, video, captcha, chat or appointment-booking services from third parties; all font, style and script files are self-hosted. If external content is embedded in the future, it will be embedded in a privacy-friendly manner wherever possible and blocked pending consent, where required.

15. Job applications

Should this website accept job applications in the future, we will process application data to carry out the application process on the basis of Section 26(1) of the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG) and Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. After the process concludes, application documents will be deleted after [period, typically six months], unless explicit consent to longer storage has been given.

16. Newsletter and social media presence

This website currently does not offer a newsletter and does not currently operate any social media presence. Upon introduction, we will supplement this notice with the mailing service provider, legal basis, logging and opt-out options, or with the respective platform and joint controllership under Article 26 GDPR.

17. Recipients and processors

Once the future project company commences business operations, personal data may be transferred to carefully selected service providers, such as credit institutions for payment processing, hosting, IT, identification, payment, communication and archiving service providers, credit reporting agencies and credit insurers, lawyers and debt collection service providers in the event of enforcement, tax advisers and auditors, as well as supervisory and tax authorities in connection with statutory obligations. Where these act as processors, agreements under Article 28 GDPR will be concluded. Specific categories of recipients: [to be added once determined].

18. Third-country transfers

Currently, no data transfers take place to countries outside the European Economic Area. Should third-country transfers occur in the future, this will only take place under the conditions of Articles 44 et seq. GDPR, for example on the basis of an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards; a copy of the safeguards can then be requested.

19. Retention period

We store personal data only as long as necessary for the respective purpose, or as long as statutory retention obligations apply. Once the future project company becomes operational, the following in particular will apply:

  • commercial- and tax-law retention periods under Section 257 of the German Commercial Code (HGB) and Section 147 of the German Fiscal Code (AO) (up to ten years)
  • retention obligations under Section 8 of the German Anti-Money Laundering Act (GwG) (five years)
  • retention obligations under the German Banking Act (KWG) and the applicable regulatory requirements
  • standard limitation periods under Sections 195 et seq. of the German Civil Code (BGB)

Specific deletion periods will be set out in a deletion policy. Server log files are automatically deleted after a short period customary for this purpose, unless security-related reasons require longer retention.

20. Your rights

  • right of access under Article 15 GDPR
  • right to rectification under Article 16 GDPR
  • right to erasure under Article 17 GDPR, insofar as no retention obligations apply
  • right to restriction of processing under Article 18 GDPR
  • right to data portability under Article 20 GDPR
  • right to object under Article 21 GDPR
  • right to withdraw consent given, with effect for the future, under Article 7(3) GDPR
  • right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority under Article 77 GDPR

To exercise these rights, an informal message to the email address named in the "Controller" section is sufficient.

21. Responsible data protection supervisory authority

Without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement. For the registered office of Müller Holding AG, this is the North Rhine-Westphalia State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (LDI NRW).

22. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, manipulation, unauthorised access and other risks. The website will be transmitted in encrypted form (SSL/TLS) once it is hosted under its own domain. Specific security details are not published in full, for protective reasons.
