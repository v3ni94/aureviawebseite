Product facts

What AUREVIA offers

Factoring is the ongoing purchase of monetary receivables from services already rendered, under a framework agreement. The seller of the receivable receives liquidity before the debtor pays in the normal course. In Germany, factoring is a licensable financial service under Section 1(1a) sentence 2 no. 9 KWG.

Target groupsDoctors, dentists, pharmacies, dental labs, physiotherapists, veterinarians, other healthcare professions (reviewed individually)
Non-recourse factoringThe factor assumes, to the agreed extent, the default risk of the purchased receivable (credit protection / del credere). AUREVIA primarily offers this model.
Recourse factoringThe default risk generally remains with the client, or a contractual right of recourse applies. May be offered alternatively for suitable constellations.
Minimum volume (example figure)around EUR 60,000 in annual receivables
Advance rate (example figure)up to 90% of the receivable amount
Payout period (example figure)within 24 hours of purchase
Onboarding duration (example figure)typically 5 to 10 business days
Contract term (example figure)12-month minimum term, cancellable monthly thereafter with 4 weeks' notice

Differentiation

What sets AUREVIA apart from other providers

  • Exclusive specialisation in receivables financing for healthcare, rather than cross-industry mass-market business.
  • Lower minimum volume threshold than most factoring providers geared towards large corporate clients.
  • A lean digital process that preserves the client's existing practice, inventory or billing structure, rather than fully outsourcing billing.
  • Shorter contract commitment than the multi-year terms typical of classic bank credit lines.
  • Clear separation of the receivable, purchase price, fees and security retention, rather than a blended calculation.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AUREVIA Factoring?

AUREVIA Factoring is a project of Müller Holding AG to build a specialised provider of receivables financing (factoring) for healthcare professions: doctors, dentists, pharmacies, dental labs, physiotherapists, veterinarians and other healthcare professions.

Is AUREVIA Factoring AG a registered company?

No. AUREVIA Factoring is a working name for a project currently in preparation. No standalone company under this name is entered in the German commercial register. The entity legally responsible for this website is Müller Holding AG, Düsseldorf Local Court, HRB 104291.

Does AUREVIA currently provide factoring services?

No. No factoring or other licensable financial services are currently provided. The licensing procedure under the German Banking Act (KWG) with BaFin is being prepared; no licence has yet been granted.

What is the difference between non-recourse and recourse factoring?

With non-recourse factoring, the factor assumes the agreed default risk of the purchased receivable. With recourse factoring, this risk generally remains with the client, or a contractual right of recourse applies. AUREVIA primarily offers non-recourse factoring.

Which professions is AUREVIA intended for?

Doctors, dentists, pharmacies, dental labs, physiotherapists, veterinarians, and other healthcare professions reviewed individually.

How does AUREVIA differ from a bank or a large billing service provider?

AUREVIA is exclusively specialised in receivables financing for healthcare, with a lower minimum volume threshold than factoring providers geared towards large corporate clients, shorter contract terms than classic bank credit lines, and a lean digital process rather than full outsourcing of billing.

Where can the legally binding information about AUREVIA be found?
