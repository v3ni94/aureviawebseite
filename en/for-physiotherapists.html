For Physiotherapists

Factoring for physiotherapists: more time for therapy. More predictability for liquidity.

Salaries go out at the end of the month, in full, on time, with no room for negotiation. Whether and when payment for this month's treatments will arrive, you don't yet know at that point. Physiotherapy practices are staff-intensive, and that is exactly why any delay in incoming payments hits directly. AUREVIA creates an additional lever here.

Starting point

Typical liquidity situations

The share of staff costs is the largest block and requires reliable solvency on a fixed date each month.

Growth means premises, equipment and staff ahead of the full ramp-up in revenue. A new therapist position costs money from day one and is only fully utilised after months.

Different payers and billing channels make cash flow hard to track: payers, other practices and self-paying patients run on different rhythms.

Continuing education is a precondition for higher-reimbursed services and is paid upfront: manual therapy, lymphatic drainage, neuro-physiotherapy (KG-ZNS), Bobath.

Self-pay and prevention offerings are growing, with direct consumer receivables and corresponding default risk.

Impact

What AUREVIA changes for you

  • Liquidity from receivables decouples your ongoing costs in time from the incoming payment.
  • More flexible growth financing, without having to finance every short-term peak on a long-term basis.
  • Payment monitoring and dunning for submitted receivables are no longer your responsibility; in small practices, this is the work that's left for the kitchen table in the evening.
  • A clear digital overview of submitted and purchased receivables.
  • A dedicated contact for special cases.
Process

How it works in an allied health practice

You continue to invoice through your usual channel, whether directly or via a billing centre. AUREVIA does not necessarily replace this; instead, it applies to the receivable itself. Submission happens in bundles in line with your billing cycle, not per treatment. For self-pay and private-patient receivables, consent is additionally documented.

One point where AUREVIA has to be precise: allied-health prescriptions can be reduced or rejected on formal grounds, a missing signature, deadlines exceeded, inconsistencies in the prescription. Credit protection (del credere) covers the debtor's insolvency and unwillingness to pay. It does not replace remuneration that never arises for formal reasons in the first place. Reviewing the prescription remains your responsibility; what factoring delivers is predictability on the timeline and relief from receivables management.

Legal framework

What is legally required

Members of the allied health professions are subject to a duty of confidentiality under Section 203 of the German Criminal Code. Assigning fee receivables against patients therefore requires their express, prior and written consent. AUREVIA provides you with vetted wording for your registration documents.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions from allied health providers

Does this also apply to occupational therapy, speech therapy and podiatry?

Yes. The billing logic is comparable; terms are set individually per practice. For these professions there is also the page Other healthcare professions.

Does factoring replace my billing centre?

Not necessarily. Both can be combined, provided your existing contracts and assignments do not conflict with this. Which setup makes sense for you depends on your current contracts.

What happens if a prescription is rejected after purchase?

If the receivable lapses for reasons not covered by credit protection, it is reassigned and offset under the contractual rules. This arrangement is explained to you explicitly before the contract is concluded.
