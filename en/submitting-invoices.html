How it works

Submit an invoice. See the terms. Receive liquidity.

Factoring should take work off your hands, not add to it. Once you have completed onboarding, almost nothing changes in your day-to-day operations: you keep writing your invoices as before. This page describes every step, including the points where experience shows things tend to get stuck.

Phase 1

One-time onboarding

Initial conversation and offer

You tell us your profession, annual receivables volume, average invoice amount and debtor structure. On this basis you receive an offer with clearly stated terms: factoring fee, interest component, advance rate, security retention, term. Duration: typically within 24 hours of your enquiry, noticeably faster than the multi-day credit pre-assessment of classic bank financing.

Register and verify identity

You set up your business account and provide the required company, representation and identification data. Typically needed: current management accounts (BWA), latest annual financial statements or income/expense statement, proof of professional licensing, an open-items list, and identification of the beneficial owners under the German Anti-Money Laundering Act (GwG). The latter is a statutory requirement and not negotiable; plan for the time it takes.

Framework agreement

The framework agreement governs the purchase procedure, limits, terms, duration, retransfer and how objections are handled. It is explained to you before signing. Read the provisions on the retransfer of receivables in particular; they set out what happens if a receivable subsequently falls away.

Set up patient or customer consent

For all professions subject to the duty of confidentiality under Section 203 of the German Criminal Code, this is the step where healthcare factoring projects most often stall. We provide you with vetted consent wording for registration forms, treatment contracts and admission documents. Your team then obtains consent at first contact going forward, and once retroactively for existing patients. Without valid consent, the assignment is void; the corresponding receivables cannot be purchased.

Technical connection

You decide how you submit: customer portal, single or batch upload, or an interface to your practice, inventory, laboratory or billing software. No software change is required.

Phase 2

Ongoing operations

Create invoice

Unchanged, in your own system. The only addition is the assignment notice with the payment instruction to the AUREVIA Factoring account, mandatory for disclosed factoring and available as a saved text block in most systems.

Upload invoice or receivable data

You select the receivable and submit the invoice along with the information needed for review, individually or as a batch submission, at your own pace.

AUREVIA reviews the receivable

We review in particular the purchase requirements, the receivable's status and content, its veracity, the debtor, due date, possible objections, whether the required consent is in place, and compliance with the agreed factoring framework. If something is missing, we get back to you with a specific statement of what is missing, not a blanket rejection. Duration: typically within 24 hours of a complete upload, often on the same day for standard cases.

Review terms transparently

Before you decide, you see the key economic figures: the purchased receivable amount, purchase price or payout, fees, security retention and any other contractual parameters. An upload alone does not trigger a chargeable purchase unless expressly agreed otherwise.

Sell the receivable

You confirm the purchase through the agreed procedure. Once the assignment is valid and the purchase requirements are met, the receivable transfers to AUREVIA.

Receive the purchase price

Payout follows the timeframe and payment logic set out in the contract: up to 90% of the receivable amount within 24 hours of purchase. The security retention is released once payment is received or once the agreed period has elapsed.

Payment processing, collections and status

Your debtor pays AUREVIA. Payment monitoring, payment reminders and collections follow the escalation levels set out in the framework agreement; you can take individual cases out of the standard process. In the portal you can see at any time the status of your submitted receivables, payouts, retentions and any information still required.

Documents

What documents are typically required?

  • Invoice or receivable data record
  • Proof of the underlying service, where required
  • Debtor and due-date information
  • Proof of consent, where the profession is subject to a duty of confidentiality
  • Supplementary billing or contract documents, where needed
  • Company and KYC documents during onboarding
Named in advance

The three most common pitfalls

1. Missing consent

The most common reason for rejected purchases in healthcare professions. Solution: make consent a fixed part of the admission process, not an exception.

2. Incomplete invoice details

Missing service data, unclear line items, missing prescription data. An invoice your debtor could dispute is also a problem for the purchase.

3. Direct payments to you

If a debtor pays into your account despite the assignment notice, the amount must be forwarded on. Report such receipts promptly; sorting it out afterwards costs both sides time.

Data protection

Data protection and sensitive health data

Following the principle of data minimisation, AUREVIA requests only the data required for purchase, risk review, processing and statutory obligations. Patient data and special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR are only processed where a valid legal basis and a correspondingly secured process exist. Details: Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the process

How long does onboarding take overall?

From the initial conversation to the first payout, typically 5 to 10 business days, depending mainly on how quickly documents and the anti-money-laundering check are completed, and generally markedly faster than a comparable credit line from a bank.

Do I have to submit every invoice individually?

No. Batch submissions and automated transmission from your software are the standard case.

Can I withdraw an invoice?

At any time before purchase. After purchase, the framework agreement's provisions on retransfer apply.

What happens with credit notes and cancellations?

These are reported and offset against the security retention or the next payout.

Who answers queries from my patients or customers?

You answer questions about the service itself; AUREVIA answers questions about payment, deadlines and payment routing. This division is set out during onboarding and explained to your team.
