For Other Healthcare Professions

Factoring for other healthcare professions: your service. Your receivable. Your room to manoeuvre.

Small structure, large upfront outlay. Not every profession in the healthcare sector has an established billing industry behind it, yet the underlying economic question is the same: the service has been provided, the invoice has been issued, and the money has not yet arrived. AUREVIA therefore also reviews receivables from other healthcare professions and health service providers on an individual basis.

Target group

Who this page applies to

  • Alternative and complementary medicine practitioners (Heilpraktiker): predominantly self-pay and supplementary-insurance receivables, high dispersion, no cost-carrier backing.
  • Speech therapy: allied-health billing with a long payment lead time and a high personnel-cost ratio.
  • Occupational therapy: comparable structure, with additional equipment and material requirements.
  • Podiatry / chiropody: high share of self-pay work alongside prescribed services.
  • Midwives and birth centres: multiple cost carriers, professional liability insurance as a substantial fixed-cost item.
  • Psychotherapy practices: private billing over long treatment periods and particularly sensitive data.
  • Home care services: monthly personnel costs against time-lagged billing.
  • Medical supply stores, orthopaedic technology and orthopaedic shoe technology: material pre-financing, approval requirements, long billing routes.
  • Hearing care / audiology retail and optometry with a supply component: a mix of retail and statutory health insurance billing.
  • Nutrition counselling, osteopathy, medical foot care and other health services with predominantly private invoicing.

Your profession isn't listed? That doesn't rule anything out. What matters is the structure of your receivables and who owes them, not your professional title.

Starting point

Typical liquidity situations

Personnel and premises costs fall due monthly, revenue does not. The higher your personnel share, the harder every delay hits.

The self-pay share is growing, and with it the default risk. A receivable against a private individual is a different matter from one against a cost carrier.

Conventional bank products are not optimally sized for small, short-term needs. Little collateral, often a freelance structure, limited balance-sheet history.

Impact

What AUREVIA changes for you

  • Individual review instead of a blanket industry approach.
  • Financing is tied to real receivables, not to collateral.
  • A digital process with personal support for special cases.
  • A clear economic comparison before you decide, even when the result speaks against factoring.
Process

How it works for smaller units

The process is the same as for larger operations, only the format differs. Numerous small receivables are handled through batch upload; a small number of large receivables require a limit check on the respective debtor. What determines suitability is your annual receivables volume, not your legal form or headcount.

Legal framework

What needs separate clarification for your profession

For all professions subject to the duty of confidentiality under Section 203 of the German Criminal Code, which includes among others psychotherapists, alternative and complementary medicine practitioners, midwives and members of the allied health professions, assigning a fee receivable requires the express, prior and written consent of the person treated. AUREVIA clarifies this for your specific situation in the initial consultation and provides the appropriate consent wording. This restriction does not apply to purely commercial receivables, for example a medical supply store's deliveries to a care facility.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I'm a sole trader with no employees. Does factoring apply to me?

What matters is your annual receivables volume, not your legal form. The minimum threshold is around EUR 60,000 in annual receivables.

My invoices are individually small but numerous. Is that a problem?

No. Small-value receivables portfolios can be handled well through batch upload or an interface.
