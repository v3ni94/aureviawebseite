For Pharmacies

Factoring for pharmacies: liquidity for stock purchasing, inventory and day-to-day operations.

The wholesaler delivers in the morning, and the invoice is in your inbox the same day. The money for the high-cost pack you dispensed yesterday arrives with a delay. Between these two dates lies your entire working capital, and in hardly any other sector does your ability to pay decide the outcome so directly for earnings. Your gross margin on prescription medicines is largely fixed; the lever that remains is purchasing.

Starting point

Typical liquidity situations

Early-payment discounts and prompt-payment terms assume you can pay when it is worthwhile. Anyone who regularly has to ask for a payment term is fundamentally negotiating different conditions.

A single high-cost medication ties up the equivalent of a whole day's revenue. With several such cases a month, a liquidity gap opens up that has nothing to do with how the business is actually performing.

Home care, nursing facilities and institutional supply mean large individual amounts and fixed billing cycles.

Supply shortages and seasonal peaks reward whoever can stock up while goods are available.

Branch networks, refurbishment, an automated dispensing system or a takeover: the stock on hand must be paid for immediately at handover.

Impact

What AUREVIA changes for you

  • More room for stock purchasing and using early-payment discounts, where it makes economic sense.
  • Financing from real receivables instead of solely from additional debt.
  • More predictable cash flows and more transparent working-capital management.
  • Digital submission and clear status information for every receivable submitted.
  • Under non-recourse factoring: transfer of the agreed default risk, in particular for commercial debtors.
Process

How it works in a pharmacy

Your prescription billing continues to run as before; AUREVIA does not replace your pharmacy billing centre. Factoring engages with the receivables arising from your home-care, nursing, institutional and commercial business, as well as other purchasable claims. Submission is ideally done as a batch upload from your inventory management system. For new commercial debtors, a limit is checked before the first purchase.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions from pharmacists

Does AUREVIA replace my pharmacy billing centre?

No. The billing centre prepares and settles your prescription billing; AUREVIA purchases receivables and provides advance financing. In principle, both can run alongside each other, provided your existing contracts and security assignments do not stand in the way.

Does this pay off against the early-payment discount advantage?

That is the right question, and it can only be answered from your own figures. What matters is your purchasing volume, your current share of early-payment discounts taken, and your average outstanding receivables. AUREVIA prepares the comparison for you on this basis.

What is the effect on my balance sheet?

Under non-recourse factoring, the receivables balance can be reduced; the specific accounting treatment should be reviewed with your tax adviser.
