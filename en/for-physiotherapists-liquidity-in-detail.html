In depth · Physiotherapists

Salaries arrive monthly, the money doesn't

Back to Factoring for Physiotherapists

Staff costs are by far the largest cost block in physiotherapy, and they fall due at the end of the month, in full, on time, with no room for negotiation. Your money arrives later: after the billing run, after review by the payers, after disbursement. Factoring shifts your incoming payments to match your treatment volume rather than the billing calendar.

The clawback risk, and the product's honest limit

Allied-health prescriptions are reduced or fully rejected on formal grounds: missing signature, deadlines exceeded, frequency requirements not met, inconsistencies in the prescription. The treatment has been provided, staff have been paid, and the remuneration falls away. Given the margins in allied health provision, every clawback hits the result directly.

This is where AUREVIA has to be precise: credit protection (del credere) covers the debtor's insolvency and unwillingness to pay. It does not replace remuneration that never arises for formal reasons in the first place. Providers who suggest otherwise are promising something no factoring agreement delivers. What factoring delivers is predictability on the timeline and relief from payment monitoring and dunning; reviewing the prescription remains your responsibility.

Where the liquidity in practices actually goes

  • Staff growth. An additional therapist position costs in full from day one and is only fully utilised after months.
  • Second location. Rent, equipment and staff ramp up before the appointment book is full.
  • Equipment and fit-out. Training area, treatment tables, physical therapy equipment, accessibility.
  • Continuing education. Manual therapy, lymphatic drainage, neuro-physiotherapy (KG-ZNS) and Bobath are a precondition for higher-reimbursed services, and are paid upfront.
  • Self-pay and prevention offerings. A growth area with direct consumer receivables and corresponding default risk.

What you get back in everyday practice

Payment monitoring, payment reminders and dunning fall away for submitted receivables. In small practices, this work is usually done by the owner themselves in the evening, time that is neither billable nor restful. For self-pay and private-patient receivables, the following also applies: the assignment requires the express, prior and written consent of the person treated. We provide vetted wording for your registration documents.

Is this worthwhile at my practice size?

This depends less on size than on your staff-cost ratio and the length of your payment terms. The minimum volume threshold is around EUR 60,000 in annual receivables, noticeably lower than at most factoring providers geared towards large corporate clients.

What happens if a claim is rejected after payout?

If the receivable lapses for reasons outside credit protection, it is reassigned and offset under the contractual rules. This arrangement is explained to you explicitly before the contract is concluded.
