The cash-discount calculation: the only comparison that matters in a pharmacy

Back to Factoring for Pharmacies

Early-payment discounts and prompt-payment terms from wholesalers relate to a payment deferral of only a few weeks. Calculated on an annual basis, a cash-discount rate therefore corresponds to a substantial effective rate of interest, considerably higher than the plain percentage suggests. Anyone who can always pay immediately also fundamentally negotiates different terms than someone who regularly has to ask for a payment term. For your pharmacy, this means the relevant question is not what factoring costs, but whether the purchasing advantage from reliable immediate payment is greater. This calculation can only be worked out on your real purchasing and revenue figures, not on illustrative sample values. AUREVIA prepares it for you on this basis.

High-cost medication ties up capital that is then missing elsewhere

A single preparation from oncology, haemophilia or immunotherapy can tie up the equivalent of an entire day's revenue. You procure it, dispense it, bill it, and wait. With several such cases a month, a liquidity gap opens up that has nothing to do with how the business is actually performing. The same applies to cytostatic compounding, home care and nursing-home supply: large individual amounts, high advance-financing needs, fixed billing cycles.

Other situations where advance financing makes the difference

  • Reimbursement clawbacks (retax). Rejected prescriptions hit liquidity retroactively and unpredictably.
  • Supply shortages. Whoever can stock up while goods are available gains a supply and earnings advantage.
  • Seasonal peaks. Cold-and-flu season and vaccination campaigns require stocking up in advance.
  • Branch networks. Multiple locations multiply the capital tied up.
  • Refurbishment and automation. Automated dispensing systems, compounding, accessibility, investments with long payback periods.
  • Succession and takeover. The stock on hand must be paid for immediately at handover.

What factoring does not do

Reimbursement clawbacks are not a matter for credit protection. If a billing item is reduced for formal reasons, the receivable lapses in whole or in part; this is not a question of the debtor's creditworthiness. Factoring improves your liquidity planning and takes receivables management off your hands; safeguarding against clawbacks remains the responsibility of your prescription review.

Does this replace my pharmacy billing centre?

No. Prescription billing continues to run as before; factoring engages with the receivables arising from it and from institutional and commercial business. Any existing security assignments must be reviewed beforehand.

What effect does this have on my bank rating?

Under non-recourse factoring, the receivables balance can be reduced, which may affect balance-sheet structure and the equity ratio. Discuss the specific accounting treatment with your tax adviser; no rating commitment can be derived from this.
