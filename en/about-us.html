About AUREVIA

We make liquidity simpler for healthcare professions.

AUREVIA Factoring is a project of Müller Holding AG to build a specialised provider of receivables financing for the healthcare market. The goal is not to copy existing banks or billing systems, but to solve a concrete task: turning open receivables into available liquidity quickly, transparently and professionally.

Focus

Why AUREVIA?

Doctors, dentists, pharmacies and other healthcare professions work in an environment with high quality requirements, regulatory complexity and distinctive payment flows. At the same time, many classic financing processes are not optimised for small, short-term liquidity needs.

A provider that finances everything rarely understands any of it in detail. The billing logic of a prosthodontic treatment has little in common with that of a medical supply store, and neither has anything to do with a pharmacy's inventory cycle. Anyone who does not know these differences miscalculates limits, asks for the wrong documents, and ends up arguing over things that should have been clarified beforehand in the event of a claim. That is why AUREVIA is meant to do exactly one thing: purchasing receivables in healthcare.

Approach

Our principles

Clarity

You should understand what is being purchased, what it costs and what happens afterwards, including the limits of credit protection.

Speed

Standard cases are consistently digitised and processed efficiently.

Responsibility

Speed does not replace risk review, compliance or data protection.

No selling against the facts

If factoring does not make economic sense in your case, we will tell you so.

Discretion

Financing is a professional management tool; we handle your information accordingly. The handling of health data is described in the Privacy Policy.

Partnership

We do not think in individual transactions, but in a resilient business relationship with fixed points of contact.

Status

Regulatory status

In Germany, factoring is a financial service under Section 1(1a) sentence 2 no. 9 of the German Banking Act (KWG). AUREVIA Factoring is currently a project of Müller Holding AG in preparation; a dedicated company has not yet been founded and is not yet entered in the commercial register. The licensing procedure under the KWG with BaFin is being prepared, no licence has yet been granted, and no factoring services are currently provided.

Note: This page serves exclusively as advance information about a project in preparation. Once structure, incorporation and licensing status are finally settled, this information will be reconciled with the Imprint and updated.
