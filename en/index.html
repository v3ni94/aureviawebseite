Factoring & Finance for Healthcare

Short-term liquidity for healthcare professions.

The service has been provided, the patient is long gone, and your money is still sitting somewhere between the invoice, reimbursement and payment. AUREVIA Factoring purchases your receivables and makes committed working capital available sooner. Digital, transparent and tailored to smaller and mid-sized financing needs.

For doctors, dentists, pharmacies, dental labs, physiotherapists, veterinarians and other healthcare professions.

The principle

Creating liquidity where it originates

An open invoice is work you have already done, just work that hasn't reached your account yet. That's exactly where factoring comes in: you sell your receivable to AUREVIA and receive the agreed purchase price earlier than through the regular payment cycle. Rent, staff, supplies and investments become plannable again, instead of depending on how quickly others choose to pay.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is factoring a loan?

No. Factoring is the purchase of a receivable under a framework agreement. The financing effect comes from the advanced purchase price, not from borrowed capital.

Will my patients or customers notice anything?

With disclosed factoring, the assignment is stated on the invoice, together with the payment instructions. In healthcare, this has been a well-established practice for decades.

Do I need a certain practice size?

What matters is your receivables volume, not your legal form or headcount. The minimum threshold is around EUR 60,000 in annual receivables, noticeably lower than at most factoring providers geared towards large corporate clients.

What does factoring cost?

Cost depends on the type of receivable, volume, term, debtor structure, risk and scope of service. The relevant terms are disclosed before every purchase.
